What each direction brings

A · Calm Ledger

Swiss precision: hairlines instead of cards, numbers doing the work.

Strengths
  • Best-disciplined colour and accessibility
  • The strongest chart: to scale, a Today marker, a working Year view, arrow-key tooltips
  • The "4.0 → 3.9 kW without June 2025" comparison
Weaknesses
  • Feels cold, with little of June's character
  • The history state drops the daily chart
  • The ≈ € button is too small to tap (14 px)

B · Energy Almanac

Editorial: a headline that answers, an 88 px numeral, one annotated chart.

Strengths
  • Best 5-second comprehension and hierarchy
  • The strongest June voice: squiggle motif, warm copy
  • Text summaries under charts, a clear CTA with context
Weaknesses
  • Popovers close on scroll (keyboard users lose them)
  • The Peak headline mixes two messages, and the Flemish comparison appears 3 times
  • The blob behind the hero is clipped, and the peak marker floats too high

C · Soft Native

App-like: large soft tiles, a live gauge instrument.

Strengths
  • Most tactile, the best dark mode
  • 12-month peak strip showing June 2025 rolling off
  • Latest and month peaks paired under the chart
Weaknesses
  • The gauge uses an invented 0–6 kW scale, and "−0.24 kW" reads as a negative reading
  • Competing big numbers (113 kWh vs 290 kWh), too much red
  • Drifts toward a generic energy app

Recommended: B as the base, plus the best of A and C

Both evaluators came to this independently. If you agree, Phase 4 applies it to all 8 tabs and every edge state.

Keep from B

  • Headline sentence + oversized light numeral
  • Squiggle underline as the single brand motif
  • Editorial column + slim side rail
  • Chart text summaries, CTA with context line

Borrow from A and C

  • A: Peak chart (Today marker, Year view, arrow-key tooltips)
  • A: "4.0 → 3.9 kW" comparison, mobile table, red discipline
  • A: "Complete after June 2026" on the history state
  • C: 12-month peak strip, sliding tab and segmented-control thumbs

Drop

  • C's gauge and its invented kW scale
  • C's 113 kWh hero (contradicts 290 kWh)
  • A's visible "New idea" badge
  • B's clipped blob and floating peak marker

Fixes to B itself

  • Popovers close only on Esc or a click outside
  • Peak headline leads with "4.0 kW, ≈ €7.40 a month, just under the Flemish average", with the comparison said once
  • Inactive tabs taken out of the keyboard order, tap targets ≥ 24 px
  • The illustrative September daily peaks logged in the demo-data change log
