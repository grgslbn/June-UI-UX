About these numbers: a peak is your highest 15-minute power use. ≈ marks the estimated capacity charge — a rough regional rate, not your grid operator's bill.

Rolling average

Each month, your single highest 15-minute peak is recorded. Your rolling average is the mean of your last 12 monthly peaks. Every new month pushes the oldest one out — that's the "rolling" part.

Capacity tariff

In Flanders, part of your grid costs depends on how much power you draw at once, not only on how much energy you use in total. Your 12-month rolling average is the number that sets it.
