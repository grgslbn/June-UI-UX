# June brand book 2018 — distilled (inspiration, not strict rules)

Source: `reference/june-brandbook-2018.pdf`, contact sheet `reference/brandbook-contact-sheet.png`.

## Personality
"Playful, trustworthy and reliable." / "Strong, playful & POWER."
Purpose: *June optimizes energy consumption and the corresponding costs to customers* — analyses and advises on the optimal domestic energy choices.
Tagline (NL): "Je had energie, nu heb je power."

## Colour
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Brand primary | June Green | `#3bada8` |
| Brand accent / contrast | June Red | `#e85257` |
| Ink (cover, logo, text) | Dark slate (from cover) | ≈ `#1b2a30` (gallery already uses `#172429`) |
| Electricity | Sunglow Yellow | `#fad145` |
| Gas | Picton Blue | `#4abae?` (hex truncated in source — use ≈ `#4ab9e8`) |
| Water | French Blue | `#3087e8` |
| Surfaces | very light mint tint + white | ≈ `#e6f5f4` / `#ffffff` |

Rule from the book: each energy "type" has its own colour; June Red can be added to any type for contrast.

## Typography
- **Montserrat** (bold / extra-bold) — titles, subtitles, attention text.
- **Open Sans** — body, subtext, long text.

## Graphic elements
- Lightning-bolt icon (hand-drawn, black with offset shadow).
- Organic **blobs** (red, mint), **halftone dot grids**, **squiggle / wave lines**, small rings and dots — in the colour of the energy type.
- Mascot **Junior** (red blob character, many emotions; sits in a bottom corner on a light background).
- Photography framed by blobs + dot patterns.

## How to translate this into a *premium minimal* product UI (coordinator guidance)
- Keep: June Green as the single interactive accent, ink as the primary text colour, Montserrat for display numerals/headings, energy-type colours for **data series only** (elec = yellow, gas = blue).
- Tone down: blobs / dots / squiggles become *rare, quiet* accents (e.g. a faint halftone in an empty state, a mint blob behind a hero number) — never decoration on every card.
- June Red = alerts, peaks, over-budget only.
- Junior can appear in empty / celebratory states only, small, at most once per screen.
- Consider Open Sans → a more modern humanist/grotesk for UI text is allowed if a direction justifies it (brand book is inspiration only).
