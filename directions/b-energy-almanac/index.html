Rolling average

Each month, your single highest 15-minute peak is recorded. Your rolling average is the mean of your last 12 monthly peaks — when a new month is added, the oldest one drops off.

Rolling average

Each month, your single highest 15-minute peak is recorded. Once you have 12 months, your rolling average is the mean of the last 12 — until then it's the average of the months you have.

Capacity tariff

Part of your Flemish grid fee depends on how much power you draw at the same moment, not only on how much energy you use in total. It's based on your 12-month rolling average peak.
