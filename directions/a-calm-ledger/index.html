Capacity tariff

Part of your Flemish electricity bill depends on how much power you draw at once (kW), not only on how much energy you use in total (kWh).

It’s based on your 12-month rolling average peak — so fewer big spikes means a lower charge.

Rolling average

We take your highest 15-minute peak from each of the last 12 months and average them. Each new month replaces the oldest — that’s the “rolling” part.

Estimated capacity charge New idea

Rough estimate, fixed regional €/kW rate — not your grid operator’s exact rate. Eliq’s price formulas can’t compute this component.

How we estimate savings

The kWh a tip saves, multiplied by an average price (average, €0.27/kWh) — not your own tariff.

Used at home

Also called self-consumption: the share of your solar production you used yourself instead of sending it to the grid.
