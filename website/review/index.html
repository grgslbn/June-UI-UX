june.energy redesign · final review

One site, built from five ideas, scored by five evaluators.

This is a complete Astro website in Dutch and French: home, plans, Switch Plus, how it works, FAQ and a working sign-up flow up to the savings estimate. It started from research by six specialists and five concepts. Four concepts were built in full and scored. The final site combines the winner (C · Honest Market) with the strongest parts of A, B and E, and was scored again.

What the final site took from each concept

From C · the base

  • Manifesto hero: "Energieleveranciers rekenen op je trouw. Wij niet."
  • Three questions answered in the hero, and who pays June
  • A market chart that draws itself, footnotes, editorial type
  • The "Wat we van je weten" panel in sign-up

From A and B

  • A: hero tile with price, guarantee and "checked monthly", the Premium band with the real leaf dongle, and postcode → town and grid operator
  • B: "what you keep" per plan (only after an estimate), per-step URLs, and answers that survive the NL↔FR switch

From E and the assets

  • One hand-drawn squiggle per page and the button hover
  • The teal June character: pointing in the FAQ, cheering on confirmation
  • The official SVG logo, logo green #1CA498, the real NL/FR app mockups and the animated brand icons
