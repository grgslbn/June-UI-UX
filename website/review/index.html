june.energy redesign · checkpoint 2 · concepts

Five ways to sell automatic switching.

Each concept is a working Astro homepage in Dutch and French, plus a style tile. All five share the same facts, rules and quality bar. They differ in idea, hero, how loud the brand is, and how they persuade. Pick 3–4 to build in full: the plans page, the Switch Plus page, how it works, the FAQ and sign-up steps 1–2.
