## 1. Positioning & messaging

### 1.1 Positioning statement
For **Belgian households** who know they pay too much for energy but have no time or appetite to keep up with the market, **June** is the **independent switching service** that **watches every contract for you and moves you to a better deal automatically**. Unlike comparison sites (one-off, and you still do the work) and suppliers (they sell their own contract), June is **paid by you, not by suppliers**, **never stops checking**, and **does the admin**.

> Internal short form: *June keeps you on the best energy deal, without you having to think about it.*

### 1.2 Brand promise
**NL:** *Altijd een goed contract. Nooit meer zelf zoeken.*
**FR:** *Toujours un bon contrat. Plus jamais besoin de chercher.*

(What we promise is **continuity and no effort**, not "the cheapest ever". The €326 average is proof, not the promise. This keeps us honest in months when the market doesn't move.)

### 1.3 Reasons to believe
1. **Independent.** June is not a supplier. The subscription is our income, so we have no reason to push one supplier.
2. **Continuous.** June analyses the market every month against *your* consumption profile (17+ suppliers `[verify]`), not once when you sign up.
3. **Automatic, on your terms.** June switches for you, or asks you first: you choose. We do all the paperwork, with no interruption to your supply.
4. **Proven.** 20.000+ households, €326 average saving a year, 4,3/5 on Google (1.200+ reviews) `[verify]`.
5. **Guaranteed (Switch Plus).** If you save less than the subscription costs, you get €99 back `[verify exact T&Cs]`.
6. **Insight (Premium).** The June Dongle reads your digital meter every 15 minutes, with solar monitoring and the Insights dashboard.
7. **Belgian.** Built for the Belgian market (digital and analogue meters, Flanders / Wallonia / Brussels). Green-energy option available.

## 2. Voice & tone

**Personality (from 2018, sharpened):** *playful, trustworthy, reliable* → on the web: **clear first, warm second, playful last**. Humour lives in the small moments (the confirmation screen, the 404 page, a Junior cameo), never in prices, terms or the guarantee.

### 2.1 Address form
- **NL: je/jij.** This is the Flemish consumer-brand norm (banks and telecoms now use it too). It matches the app and the heritage tagline. Use "u" only in legal documents if legal asks for it.
- **FR: vous.** Belgian French consumer communication about money and contracts defaults to vous. Tu reads as juvenile or French-startup and undermines trust with the 45+ bill-payer. We get warmth from short sentences and active verbs, not from tutoiement. The FR team writes original copy: no word-for-word translation, and FR copy may be up to 20% longer (layouts must allow for this).
- "We" = June as a team (wij / nous). Never "June Energy BV" in body copy.

### 2.2 Do / Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Lead with the outcome ("Je betaalt minder"), then the mechanism | Lead with technology ("Ons AI-algoritme…") |
| Use concrete numbers with their basis ("gemiddeld €326 per jaar, op basis van … `[source]`") | Use unqualified superlatives ("de goedkoopste", "altijd het laagste tarief") |
| Say what happens next on every CTA ("Bereken je besparing, in 2 minuten") | Use vague CTAs ("Ontdek meer", "Start nu!") |
| Name the price early and plainly (€5,75/maand, jaarlijks gefactureerd) | Hide the price or show it only per day to look smaller |
| Admit limits ("Als de markt niet beweegt, wisselen we niet. Dan betaal je je abonnement, en de Winstgarantie dekt je.") | Promise savings every month |
| Write short sentences, one idea each | Stack exclamation marks, use caps or emoji in UI copy |
| Keep "je" and "vous" consistent within a page | Mix "u" and "je" |

### 2.3 Words to avoid / handle with care
- **"Gratis" / "gratuit":** only for something that is actually free with no conditions. The dongle is *included in Premium* (NL "inbegrepen", FR "inclus"), **not** "gratis". A switch is "zonder extra kosten" only if that is true `[verify]`.
- **"Goedkoopste" / "le moins cher", "altijd", "gegarandeerd":** only with a qualifier or when backed by the Winstgarantie T&Cs.
- **Hype:** revolutionair, slim(ste) AI, disruptief, game-changer, magisch, "révolutionnaire", "intelligence artificielle" as a selling point, 🚀.
- **Jargon on first mention:** EAN, P1-poort, capaciteitstarief, injectie, DNB/GRD. Say it plainly first ("de poort op je digitale meter", "wat je zonnepanelen terugleveren") and explain the term on demand, the way the app does.
- **Fear:** "Je wordt opgelicht door je leverancier". June is independent, not angry.
- **"Klant" in hero copy.** Prefer "gezinnen" / "ménages" (people, not accounts).

### 2.4 Microcopy principles (shared with the app)
1. **Buttons are verbs plus an object:** "Bereken je besparing", "Kies Switch Plus", "Koppel je meter". Maximum 4 words.
2. **One way to say "estimate":** the `≈` marker plus a popover, exactly as in the app. The calculator shows "≈ €310 per jaar".
3. **Euros before kWh.**
4. **Prices always come with their period and billing:** "€8,25 per maand · jaarlijks gefactureerd". Include VAT `[verify]`.
5. **Forms:** every field says why we ask for it ("We gebruiken je postcode om je netbeheerder te kennen"). Errors say how to fix the problem, not just what went wrong.
6. **Reassurance next to the CTA, not in the footer:** "Opzeggen kan altijd" / "Résiliable à tout moment" `[verify]`, "Geen onderbreking van je levering".
7. **Numbers:** Belgian formatting in both languages. NL "€ 326" or "€326" (pick one: **€326**); FR "326 €". Decimal comma in both.
8. **Sentence case everywhere**, no Title Case in NL or FR.

## 3. Visual brand translation for the web

### 3.1 Principle
The 2018 book is a **social-media system** (loud blobs, halftones and photos on every tile). The app (Soft Native) has already shown how to turn it into a **premium quiet system**: mint canvas, white tiles, June Green as the only interactive hue, Montserrat numerals, one brand motif per screen. **The marketing site sits between the two.** It is louder than the app (it has to persuade and be remembered) but shares the app's tokens, so the product feels like the ad.

> **Volume dial:** App = 2/10 brand motif. Site default (variants A–D) = 4–5/10. Variant E = 8/10. Social = 10/10 (2018 book).

### 3.2 Logo
- Use the hand-lettered **"june" wordmark plus the bolt** as in the app header (`#brand-bolt`). Ink on light, white on June Green or ink. Never green on red.
- Clear space = the height of the "j". Minimum width 72px on screen.
- Use the stacked "wordmark + tagline + bolt" lock-up (2018 page 4) **only** in the footer or on a brand moment (Visie page, variant E hero), not in the nav.
- Favicon and app icon: bolt on June Green.
- Do not recolour the logo in the energy colours, add effects to it, or put it on a photo without a scrim.

### 3.3 Colour roles (web)
Use the **app tokens as the source of truth** (`app/tokens.css`). The site adds a *marketing layer* on top.

| Role | Token / hex | Web use |
|---|---|---|
| Canvas | `--canvas #edf4f3` / white | Page background alternates mint canvas ↔ white sections (no grey) |
| Ink | `--ink-900 #14232a` | Text; **ink-block sections** (dark bands for proof/testimonials, footer) |
| Interactive | `--accent-strong #19736f` | Buttons, links, focus (passes AA on white). **The only CTA colour.** |
| Brand fill | `--accent #3bada8` | Large brand fields, illustrations, "your savings" in charts, Junior's world. Never used as text on white (it fails contrast). |
| June Red | `#e85257` (graphic) / `#c4383e` (text) | **Marketing accent, used sparingly:** one red blob, a highlight or Junior. Never a CTA and never an error (except on forms). The app reserves red for alerts, so on the site red = *brand spark*, not *warning*. Keep the two uses visually distinct: small blobs vs. text plus icon. |
| Electricity / Gas | `--elec #f0b429` / `--gas #1f86c4` | Only when talking about that energy type (calculator toggles, tariff charts, product illustrations). Never as a section background. |
| Water (French Blue) | `#3087e8` | **Retired** from the web (June does not sell water services). Keep it in the archive. |
| Mint soft | `--accent-soft #e2f3f1`, `--ambient #b6e3df` | Blob fills, card highlights, pricing "recommended" wash |

Dark mode: the site honours `prefers-color-scheme` using the app's dark tokens (the continuity bonus is that product screenshots match). Marketing imagery needs a dark-safe variant or a surface card behind it.

### 3.4 Typography
- **Keep Montserrat and switch the body face to Inter**, the same as the app, to replace Open Sans. Inter is sharper at small sizes, has tabular figures and matches the product one-to-one. Open Sans stays only in the 2018 archive.
- **Montserrat roles:** display headlines (600–700; 800 **only** in variant E), all big numerals (tabular, `--tracking-display -0.02em`).
- **Inter roles:** everything else. Body 17–18px on the marketing site (larger than the app's 16px for long reading), UI 14–16px.
- **Scale (site):** 14 / 16 / 18 / 22 / 32 / 48 / 72 (hero on desktop; 40–44 on mobile). Headline line-height 1.05–1.1. Maximum ~60 characters per line for body text.
- Self-host both faces (subset Latin plus Latin-ext for FR diacritics), `font-display: swap`, and preload only the display weight used in the hero.
- Variant E may add a hand-drawn accent (the wordmark's lettering style) for **one word per page** as an SVG, never a font.

### 3.5 Photography & illustration
- **Photography:** real Belgian homes and people *at ease*: dinner table, a kid on the couch, someone reading while the washing machine runs. Recognisable Belgian architecture (rijwoning, fermette, Brussels appartement). Natural light, warm, slightly desaturated, mint and neutral wardrobe. **Avoid:** stock "happy family pointing at a laptop", light bulbs, piggy banks, power lines, sockets or anyone holding a bill looking shocked.
- **Framing:** a photo may sit on **one** organic blob or rounded rectangle (`--r-lg 24px`), with **one** halftone patch overlapping a corner (the 2018 photo-framing device, reduced to one element).
- **Illustration:** flat, geometric, from the blob vocabulary. Used for explainers (steps, meter, dongle) in 2–3 colours: accent plus ink plus one energy colour. No 3D, no isometric clutter, and no gradient-mesh "AI" illustration.
- **Product imagery:** real app screenshots from `shots/final/` in a device frame or floating tile. **Always the real product, never a mock UI with invented numbers.**

### 3.6 Motif rules
| Motif | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Blob** | Maximum 1 large blob per viewport (behind a photo, a hero numeral or Junior). Mint by default; red only as a small accent blob (≤ 64px). |
| **Halftone dots** | One patch per section, in a corner, never behind text (same rule as the app's `.motif`). |
| **Squiggle / wave** | Only as an underline under one key word in a headline, or as a section divider in variant E. Stroke 2px, accent or elec. |
| **Lightning bolt** | Only in the logo, the favicon and a "switch happened" moment. Never as a bullet icon. |
| **Rings / dots** | Available for data-like decoration (the calculator result, a "live" indicator). |
| General | At most **2 motif types per viewport**. Every motif is `aria-hidden` SVG, never raster. |

### 3.7 Motion principles
Use the app's tokens (`--ease-out cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`, 90/160/240/480/700ms, no overshoot). Web additions:
1. **One signature motion per page** (defined per variant below). Everything else is a fade plus an 8–16px rise on scroll-in, 240–480ms, and runs once.
2. **Numbers count up once**, on first view, in ≤ 700ms. Currency is shown formatted during the count.
3. **No scroll-jacking, no parallax on text, no autoplaying video with sound.** A hero video (if any) is ≤ 12s, loops, is muted, has a poster frame and pauses off-screen.
4. `prefers-reduced-motion` → opacity only, and numbers appear at their final value.
5. Motion budget: the hero is interactive < 2.5s LCP on 4G. Motion never delays the CTA.

### 3.8 Junior (mascot)
- Junior is an **accent, not a spokesperson**. Allowed: the sign-up confirmation ("Top! We zijn gestart"), 404, FAQ empty search, the newsletter block, and variant E more broadly. Always small, bottom corner, on a light background (as the 2018 book says), and once per page at most (variant E: once per section).
- **Needs a redraw** as vector SVG in the current palette, with 4 expressions: happy, thinking, celebrating, sleeping (for "we're watching, you can relax").
- Never next to prices, the guarantee or legal copy. Never animated for longer than 1.2s.

### 3.9 Marketing site ↔ app continuity
- **Shared tokens:** the site imports `app/tokens.css` (or a copy synced from it) and adds a `site.css` layer for marketing scale (bigger type, section rhythm and motifs). No second green, no second radius set.
- **Shared components:** the `≈` estimate marker plus popover, the status chip, segmented control, primary/secondary button, surface tile and focus ring look identical on the site and in the app.
- **Same vocabulary:** "Mijn plan", "Winstgarantie", "Inzichten", "≈", "Bijgewerkt". The pricing page names features exactly as the app does.
- **Handoff:** after sign-up, the confirmation page uses the app shell header (wordmark + bolt, mint canvas), so the step from site to app feels like a continuation rather than a context switch. The "Login" button in the nav is a quiet secondary button.
- **Screenshots stay truthful:** they come from the app build (`node tools/shoot.mjs`), in light and dark mode, re-shot whenever the app changes.

### A · Calm Confidence
**Idea:** *The best energy decision is the one you never have to make again.* A premium, almost silent site that feels like the Insights app scaled up to a story. It is confident enough not to shout.

**Persona emphasis:** the busy dual-income family (35–50, house in Flanders or Brussels) who values peace of mind over maximum savings. Secondary: the risk-averse Switch Plus buyer.

**Hero:**
- NL: **Altijd het juiste energiecontract. Zonder er nog aan te denken.**
- FR: **Toujours le bon contrat d'énergie. Sans même y penser.**
- Subline plus a CTA "Bereken je besparing". Beside it, one **hero tile** in the app's hero contract: status chip "Gecontroleerd deze maand", a one-line sentence, the numeral "≈ €326 / jaar" with an ambient mint blob behind it. Below: 4,3★ · 20.000+ gezinnen.

**Visual language:** app tokens 1:1, with a mint canvas and white `--r-lg` tiles. Montserrat 600 headlines at 56–64px with generous white space (section padding 128px desktop). Imagery: one large calm home photo per page in a rounded frame, plus the one halftone corner. Motifs: ambient blob only (like the app). The CTA is `--accent-strong`, and red appears nowhere except a tiny Junior on confirmation.

**Motion signature:** *the monthly check*. The hero chip ticks from "Bezig met vergelijken…" to "✓ Gecontroleerd" (a check icon, not emoji) with a single 700ms count-up of the numeral. Scroll reveals are fades only.

**Conversion hypothesis:** trust and low cognitive load produce the highest *qualified* start rate and the lowest sign-up abandonment. Visitors who already believe that switching saves money need permission to stop worrying, not more persuasion.

**Risks:** it could read as generic fintech or "too quiet" and lose memorability. The savings number may not motivate price-driven visitors. Mitigation: the hero numeral and the photo carry the warmth.

**References:** Monzo / Revolut marketing pages (calm product-led), Apple Health pages, Linear.app (restraint), the June Insights Soft Native app itself.

### B · Savings First
**Idea:** *Your number, in 30 seconds.* The hero **is** the tool. Visitors give 2–3 inputs and see an estimate before any sales copy. The rest of the page explains "how we get you that number".

**Persona emphasis:** the price-driven saver who arrived from a Google search for "energie vergelijken" or "fournisseur énergie moins cher" and is comparing June to comparison sites (Switch entry plan).

**Hero:**
- NL: **Hoeveel betaal jij te veel? Reken het uit in 30 seconden.**
- FR: **Combien payez-vous en trop ? Calculez-le en 30 secondes.**
- Inputs: postcode · housing type (segmented control: appartement / rijwoning / halfopen / open) · electricity only / electricity + gas · optional "ik ken mijn verbruik" (kWh). The result card shows **"≈ €310 per jaar"** with a `≈` popover explaining the basis, the net saving after the subscription and a CTA "Start met June". Illustrative, method disclosed `[needs pricing/legal sign-off on estimation method]`.

**Visual language:** a white canvas with an **ink result card** (dark `--ink-900` tile, big Montserrat numeral in white, accent underline). Elec/gas colours appear **only** in the energy-type toggle and the breakdown bar. Type: Montserrat 700 for the result numeral at 72–96px and Inter for the form. Imagery is minimal (none in the hero), with one small photo in the testimonial band. Motifs: the dot/ring motif around the result only.

**Motion signature:** *the number resolves*. When inputs change, the result counts up and the bar splits into "wat je nu betaalt" (ink) vs. "met June" (accent) in 480ms. There is no motion before the visitor interacts.

**Conversion hypothesis:** a personalised number creates commitment (endowment effect). Starting sign-up with pre-filled answers raises the start rate and makes step 1 of the funnel feel already done.

**Risks:** estimate credibility and legal exposure. If the number is low or zero, the visitor bounces, so we need an honest fallback ("Je zit al goed. June houdt het zo."). The calculator needs data or an API that doesn't exist yet, so it may ship with averages by housing type. It can look like the comparison sites June wants to differ from.

**References:** Wise fee calculator, Bolt / Octopus Energy quote flow, Mortgage calculators on Hypotheek sites, Nerdwallet result cards.

### C · Honest Market
**Idea:** *The energy market is designed to be confusing. We're not.* An editorial, magazine-like site that explains how Belgian energy pricing works (fixed vs. variable, loyalty penalty, network costs) and positions June as the independent guide that acts on it for you. It is independence- and transparency-led, and it gives the Visie page and the mission a front-row seat.

**Persona emphasis:** the sceptic or researcher (often 45+, often FR-speaking Wallonia/Brussels, often a previous bad experience with a door-to-door seller or a comparison site) who needs to understand the mechanism and the business model before trusting anyone.

**Hero:**
- NL: **Energieleveranciers rekenen op je trouw. Wij niet.**
- FR: **Les fournisseurs comptent sur votre fidélité. Pas nous.**
- Subline: "June is geen leverancier. Jij betaalt ons, dus wij werken voor jou." Beside it, an **annotated chart**: "Hoe een contract duurder wordt als je niets doet" (illustrative, direct-labelled in the app's chart style, with the source cited) `[needs real CREG/VREG data]`.

**Visual language:** an off-white "paper" canvas (#f7f8f6, a near-mint tint) with ink text. Serif-free but editorial: Montserrat 600 for headlines with **Inter 18–20px body** set in a narrow reading column, pull-quotes, footnotes and numbered sources. Colour: mostly ink, with June Green for data and links. Energy colours appear in charts only, and red **only** for one "loyalty penalty" annotation. Imagery: documentary portraits of real customers (quote plus name plus town) and no stock. Motifs: the squiggle used as a hand-drawn annotation mark on charts (circling a number), and nothing else.

**Motion signature:** *the chart draws itself*. The line draws on scroll (700ms), then an annotation squiggle circles the gap. Everything else is static, as in print.

**Conversion hypothesis:** for high-intent but sceptical visitors, explaining the market and the business model ("who pays June?") removes the main objection. The start rate is lower at the top but completion and retention are higher. It also wins SEO (articles, a glossary, "prijzen" explainers).

**Risks:** it's long, so it's slow to reach the CTA (mitigate with a sticky CTA and a summary box at the top of each article). It needs real, citable market data and ongoing editorial effort. The tone can slip into accusation (see §2.3 "fear"). It's less differentiated on mobile if the charts get too small.

**References:** The Pudding / FT visual explainers, Stripe Press, Octopus Energy "Agile" explainers, Test-Aankoop / Test-Achats editorial layouts (in credibility, not in look).

### D · Product Showcase
**Idea:** *See your energy. Then let June act on it.* The site sells the **experience**: the Insights app, the dongle and live data. Switching is the engine and Insights is the visible proof. It drives upsell to **Premium + Dongle** and makes the €16,50 plan feel like the obvious "full" choice.

**Persona emphasis:** the tech-curious homeowner with a digital meter, often with solar panels or an EV, who wants control and data (Premium). Secondary: Switch Plus buyers upgrading.

**Hero:**
- NL: **Zie wat je huis verbruikt. June zorgt voor de beste prijs.**
- FR: **Voyez ce que consomme votre maison. June s'occupe du meilleur prix.**
- Visual: the **real Insights app** (Overview panel, light mode, from `shots/final/`) in a laptop and phone composition. A floating dongle render sits beside it. A chip in the hero reads "Live · elke 15 minuten". CTA "Bekijk Premium" plus a secondary "Of begin met Switch".

**Visual language:** a **dark ink hero** (app dark tokens: `#0a1315` canvas) so the light app screenshots glow, then light sections below. Type: Montserrat 600 and Inter. The product vocabulary, chips and `≈` markers match the app exactly. Energy colours are prominent (they are *data* here: elec yellow, gas blue, solar in the elec family). Imagery: product renders (dongle on a meter in a real Belgian meter cupboard), UI crops with callouts, one lifestyle photo (a phone on a kitchen counter). Motifs: none in the hero, with the halftone used only as a "signal" texture behind the dongle.

**Motion signature:** *the live tile*. The Peak/Overview tile animates a bar chart growing and a "Nu" marker pulsing once, and the app's tab bar auto-cycles through 3 tabs (Overview → Solar → Advice) at 4s each, pausing on hover or focus.

**Conversion hypothesis:** showing the tangible product raises perceived value, so a larger share of sign-ups choose Premium or Switch Plus and average revenue per user rises. A "compare plans" table directly under the showcase converts the visitor who is ready to buy.

**Risks:** it can overshadow the core promise (automatic switching) and scare away non-technical visitors with an analogue meter, so the Switch path must stay one click away. It depends on the app screenshots being current and localised (NL/FR app UI needed, **the current shots are English**). Dongle photography doesn't exist yet. Dark heroes are harder to keep AA-compliant with brand green.

**References:** Tibber and Homey marketing pages, Apple product pages (feature scroll), Oura ring site, Linear feature sections.

### E · Bold June
**Idea:** *Je had energie, nu heb je power.* The 2018 brand book at full volume: a confident, playful, unmistakably June site that nobody would mistake for a bank. Big type, June Green fields, red blobs, halftones, squiggles and Junior. It is memorable first and polished second, but still premium in craft (spacing, type, performance).

**Persona emphasis:** young renters and first-time buyers (25–38), urban, mobile-first, who switch brands easily and share on social. Also the brand-awareness and referral audience.

**Hero:**
- NL: **Je had energie. Nu heb je power.** (the heritage tagline, reinstated as the hero)
- FR: **Vous aviez de l'énergie. Maintenant, vous avez le pouvoir.**
- Subline: "June wisselt automatisch naar een beter contract. Jij doet niks." Visual: a June Green full-bleed field, a 96–120px Montserrat 800 headline in white with **"power"** underlined by a hand-drawn elec-yellow squiggle, and a large red blob with a halftone patch framing a portrait photo. Junior peeks in from the bottom-right corner.

**Visual language:** June Green as a **surface** (it's the only variant that does this), alternating with white and ink bands. Red blobs, yellow squiggles and mint halftones follow the 2018 rules (the colour of the energy type in context). Type: Montserrat 700–800 display (tight, -0.03em), Inter body, and the one hand-lettered word per page. Photography: bold, saturated portraits and moments framed by blobs (per 2018 pages 13–14), with a stronger crop than the other variants. The CTA on the green field is a **white button with ink text** (contrast-safe), and on white it is the standard `--accent-strong`.

**Motion signature:** *the blob breathe + bolt*. Hero blobs morph slowly (a 12s loop, paused under reduced motion). On CTA hover the bolt "charges" (a stroke-draw in 240ms). Junior waves once on first load (≤ 1.2s).

**Conversion hypothesis:** distinctiveness drives recall, social sharing and referral (Partnervoordelen), and makes June the brand people search for by name. That lowers acquisition cost over time, even if the direct start rate is similar to A.

**Risks:** it can feel childish or "cheap" to older bill-payers and cuts against the app's quiet premium look (a continuity gap). Green-field text contrast is tight: white on `#3bada8` is only ~2.9:1, so **large text only, ≥ 24px bold, or darken the field to `#2a938e`** `[validate AA]`. Motif overload is a risk, so it still obeys the 2-motif-per-viewport rule. It needs an illustrator and a Junior redraw.

**References:** Mailchimp (2018 rebrand), Oatly, Monzo early campaigns, Duolingo (mascot discipline), Octopus Energy (Constantine mascot), June brand book 2018.
